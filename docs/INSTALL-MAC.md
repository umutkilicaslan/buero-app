# Install Büro on your Mac (no Terminal needed)

Büro runs only on your own Mac. There is no account and nothing goes to the internet.
It works on Macs with an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4 …) and macOS 12 or newer.
Not sure? Apple menu  → About This Mac → "Chip" should say "Apple M…".

## 1. Get the file

You get a file called **Buero.dmg** (or a ZIP with it inside) from the person who shared Büro
with you, e.g. by AirDrop, e-mail or a shared folder. If it is a ZIP, double-click it first.

## 2. Install

1. Double-click **Buero.dmg**. A window with **Büro** and **Applications** opens.
2. Drag **Büro** onto **Applications**.
3. Close the window. In Finder's sidebar, click the eject button next to "Büro".

## 3. Open it the first time

Büro is not from the App Store, so macOS asks once if you trust it:

1. Open **Applications** and double-click **Büro**. macOS says it cannot check the app. Click **Done**
   (or **OK**).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Scroll down to the message about "Büro" and click
   **Open Anyway**. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
3. Double-click **Büro** again and click **Open**.

You only do this once. From now on Büro opens with a normal double-click.

## 4. Using it

- Your browser opens Büro. If you close the tab, click the little Büro icon in the **menu bar**
  (top right of the screen) → **Open Büro**.
- To stop Büro: menu bar icon → **Quit Büro**.
- Start with **Settings**: your name, address, tax number and bank details. They appear on your invoices.

## Your data

Everything is saved in the folder **Documents → Büro** on your Mac (menu bar icon → **Show my data
folder**). Back it up like your other important files, e.g. with Time Machine.

To remove Büro, drag it from Applications to the Bin. Your data folder stays until you delete it yourself.

## Something does not work?

- **"Büro is damaged and can't be opened"**: the download was blocked. Ask for the file again and copy it
  with AirDrop or a USB stick, then repeat step 3.
- **Nothing happens when I double-click**: look for the Büro icon in the menu bar and click **Open Büro**.
- **The invoice preview shows a red note**: please tell the person who shared Büro with you and send a
  screenshot.
